Understanding Your Combat System: A Quick Guide

Welcome to the thrilling world of combat within our web app! Our system is designed to provide an exciting and strategic battle experience that is both comprehensive and easy to understand. Here's a bird's-eye view of how your valiant teams clash in the digital arena:
Choose Your Heroes:Choose Your Heroes: Take a look at the enemy's gear and pick your characters wisely to match up against them.
Set Your Team: Once you've chosen your characters, the battle is all set to unfold automatically.
Await the Outcome: After your team is locked in, sit back and relax. The results of the combat will be delivered to you at midnight, you can then gather either your rewards or lose your gear
Team Assembly: Before the clash of steel, your team of characters is arrayed against the enemy. Each warrior's health (their vitality) and armor (their protective gear) are tallied up to form the team's total strength.
Commencement: As swords are drawn and spells are woven, our system smartly organizes your team by their robustness and defenses. This setup ensures that your team is ready to engage in the most strategic order.
Strength in Numbers: In the fray, every shield and every healing spell counts. We calculate the combined might of your team's armor and health, creating a pool of endurance that will sustain you through the battle.
The Power of Unity: Whether you march into battle with a small, elite force or an army, the size of your party is crucial. It can influence the tides of war and is an integral part of your strategy.
The Clash: Each character is an arsenal, equipped with weapons that not only strike hard but also possess unique abilities that can turn the tide of battle. From the swift riposte of a Shortsword to the crushing blow of a Warhammer, each weapon brings a special flavor to the combat.

That's the essence of combat! But if you're a tactician hungering for more details, read on as we delve into the nuances of our combat system.

Detailed Combat Mechanics


Team Statistics Calculation
Every battle begins with numbers. Your team's effectiveness in combat is determined by the total health and armor points of all characters combined. Think of it as measuring the overall stamina and resilience of your team before the battle horn sounds.
Health Points (HP): The lifeblood of your team. If this reaches zero, it's game over. So, your characters' combined health points are like a big shield protecting your chance of victory.
Armor Points: This is your first line of defense. Armor points absorb the blows that would otherwise reduce your health. The total armor of your team can mean the difference between a scratch and a fatal wound.
Each character's health and armor contribute to the team's totals. Moreover, the number of fighters you bring to the field can have strategic implications. A larger team may have more health overall, but remember — quality often trumps quantity.

Character Prioritization
The order of combat is not left to chaos. Characters attack rolls is ordered by their attack value of their weapons, meaning that defending effects like shields deflect the highest damage

Pseudo-Death System
The combat has a 'pseudo-death' system which means when the amount of armor reaches a threshold represented by the total of the hp and armor of A character, the death count increment by one. The death count is a realitive gameplay element that is used to trigger different abilities of different weapons or enemies

Weapons and Abilities
Each weapon in your arsenal is not just a tool for damage. They come with unique abilities that can surprise your foes. For example, a Shortsword isn't just for stabbing; it has a chance to strike twice with its Riposte ability. Knowing when and how to use these abilities can be the key to your conquest.
Ranged Weapons : Weapons that have the type 'Ranged' has the ability to reduce by 50% the highest incoming attack IF there is in your team at least one Melee weapon user, when correled with the death count, if the count is equal to the number of ranged characters it means that no melee characters is left

Shields
Characters equipped with shields have their attack damage reduced by 25% if a weapon is equipped but has the ability to deflect completly an incoming attack with a certain percentage chance. Their can be two types of reduction : Melee and Ranged that can have different chance percentage to deflect depending on the shield
